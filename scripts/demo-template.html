2. H reveals a hidden phase difference

Measuring either |+⟩ or |−⟩ directly gives 0 and 1 with equal probability. After H, amplitudes add or cancel through interference, making the states distinguishable.

Before H

0: 50%
1: 50%

After H

In Level 2, apply H to all three data qubits, inject Z into that encoded state, then apply H again. Since HZH = X, the phase error becomes a bit flip. Perform X recovery in the restored computational basis.

3. X alone cannot fully correct Y

Start with |+⟩ and inject Y on the middle data qubit. Correcting with X alone still gives equal 0/1 probabilities, but leaves |−⟩, which is orthogonal to the original state.
