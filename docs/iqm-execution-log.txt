WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT CONTAIN

The comparison log contains its actual requested server/QPU, suite, shots,
returned job ID, and the displayed analysis JSON. It does not display the
complete comparison job.json, individual comparison counts, submitted native
instructions, live layout, or run-time calibration ID. Individual dashboard
counts are retained separately and independently reproduce the displayed gain.
The older pilot's displayed raw_counts are Qiskit syn1syn0 strings. They are
exactly the reversed-character histograms of its dashboard export; do not mix
the dashboard and SDK string conventions.
